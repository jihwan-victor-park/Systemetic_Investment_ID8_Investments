# ID8 Deal Fit Rubric

Score each of the five dimensions below from 1 to 4 using the anchors in the
table. All five are equally weighted. The average of the five scores is the
fit score:

- **≥ 3.0 → High Quality**
- **≥ 3.3 → Very High Quality**

| Dimension | Score 1 | Score 2 | Score 3 | Score 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead / Round Dynamics** | Insider/CVC, bridge, SPV lead, secondary | Top-tier inside round, or near-top-tier new-money Series B+, top-tier syndicate | Top-tier inside round, or near-top-tier new-money Series B+, top-tier syndicate | New-money top-tier lead, primary, Series B+ |
| **AI Score** | No AI | — | — | Pure-play AI |
| **Fundamentals** | Sub-$5M revenue, sub-40% growth, NDR < 100%, LTV/CAC < 2x, burn > 100% of revenue, weak value prop | — | Over $25M revenue, over 70% growth, NDR > 110%, LTV/CAC > 3x, burn < 50% of revenue | Over $50M revenue growing over 100% YoY, NDR > 130%, LTV/CAC > 5x, burn < 20% of revenue, strong value prop / competitive advantage |
| **Return Potential** | < 2x or ~10% IRR expected, high risk of capital loss | 2-3x potential | > 3x potential, with some risk of capital loss | > 7x potential (or ~30% IRR), with little risk of capital loss |
| **Terms** | > 14% carry or > 5% total management fee | Within a few points of 0/10 carry/fee, multi-layer SPVs, low time horizon or little data | 0/10 SPV, reasonable time horizon and some data | Better than 0/10, reasonable time horizon and good data |

Notes on the table above, exactly as the original rubric defines it:

- **Lead / Round Dynamics**, scores 2 and 3 share the same anchor text. Use
  the strength of the evidence to place a deal at 2 vs 3 within that band.
- **AI Score** and **Fundamentals** have blank cells (no anchor was written
  for AI Score 2-3, or Fundamentals 2). Use the surrounding anchors to judge
  the right point on the 1-4 scale — a blank cell is not a forced 1 or 4. For
  AI Score, 2-3 covers a spectrum from "AI as a meaningful feature" to
  "AI is central but not the entire value prop." For Fundamentals, 2 sits
  between the Score 1 floor and the Score 3 bar on most metrics.

Be skeptical. No evidence found for a dimension means a low score, not a
guess. Never fabricate a metric or a source.
