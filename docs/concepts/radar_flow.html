Hub concept — three screens, one flow

From a partner's VC to this week's Hot Deals

The VCs tab merges each partner's own contacts with the existing Tier 1 list. Opening a VC shows what's already known (Portfolio) next to what's new (News). Anything new clears the same 3.0 gate as everything else and lands in one shared Hot Deals tab — regardless of whether it came from a partner, a Tier 1 VC, or the regular automated screen.
