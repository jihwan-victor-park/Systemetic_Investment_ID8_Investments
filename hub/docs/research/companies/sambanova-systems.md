**Rationale**

SambaNova Systems is a US-based, late-stage AI infrastructure company squarely within ID8’s geographic and Series B+ mandate, with a new-money Series F led by General Atlantic at an $11B valuation and broad institutional participation.[[1]](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ai-chip-startup-sambanova-valued-102253770.html)[[2]](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/sambanova-ai-chip-funding-valuation.html)[[4]](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/sambanova-draws-1b-at-11b-valuation-in-series-f-first-close/) The deal aligns strongly with ID8’s thesis: Tier 1+ new-money lead, deeply structural AI moat at the hardware and systems layer, and an experienced founding team with significant domain credibility and fundraising history.[[3]](https://globalventuring.com/university/sambanova-processes-250m-series-c/)[[6]](https://sacra.com/c/sambanova-systems/) Fundamentals and return potential look solid but are partly inferred from funding scale and positioning rather than disclosed metrics, and terms are likely to come through an SPV with non-baseline economics, so further work would need to focus on revenue scale, growth and exact access structure before committing.
