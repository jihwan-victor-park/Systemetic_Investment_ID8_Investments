**Rationale**

Warp passes the stage and geography gates: it is raising a Series B round and is headquartered in New York City in a developed North American market.[[1]](https://www.techcompanynews.com/warp-raises-60-million-in-series-b-funding-round/)[[2]](https://www.warp.co/b) The deal partially aligns with ID8’s thesis in that the round is a primary Series B led by a reputable institutional investor (Battery Ventures), but there is no clear evidence that Battery is a first-time, new-money Tier 1 lead joining the cap table, and Battery itself is not universally categorized as a Tier 1 benchmark name, so strict thesis alignment on 'new-money Tier 1' is not met.[[2]](https://www.warp.co/b)[[3]](https://app.fundz.net/fundings/warp-funding-round-series-b-95b6b5) Overall, Warp looks like a promising pure-play AI HCM/payroll platform with potential upside but currently limited transparency on fundamentals and terms, making this a moderate-fit rather than a clear high-quality ID8 target based on available public data.
