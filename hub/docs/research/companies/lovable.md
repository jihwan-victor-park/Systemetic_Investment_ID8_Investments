**Rationale**

Lovable is an AI-native software creation platform that has scaled to roughly $200M ARR within 12 months and just raised a $330M **Series B** at a $6.6B valuation, co-led by CapitalG and Tier‑1 Menlo Ventures with a deep strategic AI/infrastructure syndicate (NVentures, Salesforce, Databricks, DST, EQT, etc.).[[2]](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/lovable-raises-330m-series-b-at-6-6b-valuation/)[[3]](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/18/google-and-n.html)[[4]](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lovable-dev_announcing-lovable-raised-330m-at-a-66b-activity-7407404400589213696-WCM4)[[5]](https://menlovc.com/perspective/software-creation-for-all-leading-lovables-330m-series-b/)[[6]](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ericlay-virio_lovable-just-raised-a-330m-series-b-round-activity-7408908496315981824-cKof) The deal strongly aligns with ID8’s thesis on **new-money Tier 1-led Series B AI companies** and an AI-structural moat, with fundamentals and lead dynamics both in the highest rubric band; the main open questions are detailed unit economics, long‑term competitive differentiation vs. foundation-model platforms, and specific co-invest terms for ID8. Overall fit is strong for deep-dive diligence rather than watch-listing.

*Confidence: medium*
