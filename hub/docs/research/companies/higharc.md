**Rationale**

Higharc is a Durham, NC–based homebuilding AI company that just raised a $95M Series C led by Tier 1 investor Insight Partners, clearly within ID8’s North America and Series B+ mandate.[[1]](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/higharc-95m-series-c-insight-homebuilding-2026)[[4]](https://www.higharc.com/newsroom/higharc-95m-series-c-for-homebuilding-ai)[[6]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/higharc-raises-95m-series-c-to-scale-ai-for-homebuilding-302814598.html) The deal aligns strongly with ID8’s thesis: a new-money Tier 1 lead at Series C, and a product whose moat is genuinely AI-driven via proprietary spatial AI and floor-plan intelligence spanning design, estimating, sales and construction documentation, now extending into the materials supply chain through partners like US LBM.[[3]](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/higharc-lands-95m-series-c-taps-us-lbm-for-ai-estimating)[[4]](https://www.higharc.com/newsroom/higharc-95m-series-c-for-homebuilding-ai)[[5]](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/01/homebuilding-ai-startup-higharc-bags-90m-series-c-funding/)[[8]](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mbreiner_higharc-secures-95m-series-c-for-homebuilding-activity-7477745137733963776-vR7q) Fundamentals and terms are under-disclosed, so this pass rests on market/qualitative signals and investor quality rather than hard metrics, pulling the overall fit down slightly but without triggering any hard auto-pass conditions.
