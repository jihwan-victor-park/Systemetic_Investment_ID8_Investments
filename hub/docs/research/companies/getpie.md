**Rationale**

Pie (PieTech) is an AI-enabled advertising and customer acquisition platform for small businesses that recently raised a $19.5M Series A led by Tier 1+ fund Lightspeed Venture Partners, headquartered in the U.S. (Houston/NY footprint) and thus inside ID8’s North America mandate.[[4]](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/information-technology/pie-raises-19-5m-to-expand-ai-platform-for-small-businesses/) The deal aligns with ID8’s thesis on AI focus and Tier 1 new-money leadership, but it is out of current mandate on stage because it is a Series A rather than Series B+, so this looks more like a promising future candidate than a present fit. Missing financial and deal-structure data keep Fundamentals and Terms at 2 and make the overall fit preliminary rather than fully evidenced.
