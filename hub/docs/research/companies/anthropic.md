**Rationale**

Anthropic is a San Francisco–based, late-stage foundation-model company squarely within ID8’s geographic and AI mandate, with a Tier 1+/Tier 1 investor stack and a recent Series F led by ICONIQ, satisfying the thesis requirement of top-tier new money at Series B+.[[6]](https://www.texau.com/profiles/anthropic)[[8]](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-raises-series-f-at-usd183b-post-money-valuation) The founding team has elite AI safety pedigree from OpenAI, and the company appears to have scaled into a leading frontier model provider, but core financial metrics remain undisclosed, making the fundamentals and return profile strong but not fully verifiable at current very high valuations.[[1]](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-raises-series-b-to-build-safe-reliable-ai)[[2]](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/09/anthropic-closes-in-on-20b-round/)[[6]](https://www.texau.com/profiles/anthropic) Overall, the deal aligns with ID8’s AI and lead-investor thesis, though late-stage valuation and competitive intensity temper return expectations into the 3–6x range rather than a clearly 6x+ base case.
