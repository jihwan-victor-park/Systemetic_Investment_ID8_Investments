**Rationale**

Pocket is an AI hardware company building a dedicated personal assistant device with very strong early fundamentals (reported ~$27M annualised revenue and rapid unit growth) and a clearly AI-centric moat, supported by a seed round led by Tier 1+ investor Accel with YC and top-tier operator participation.[[1]](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/pocket-raises-11m-accel-and-others-demand-surges-its-personal-ai-assistant)[[2]](https://techfundingnews.com/pocket-11m-accel-ai-hardware-27m-arr/)[[4]](https://aipressroom.com/pocket-11m-ai-hardware/) The deal aligns well with ID8’s AI thesis and Tier 1 lead preference, but it breaks the mandate on stage: this is a seed/early round, not Series B+, and there is no published evidence yet on terms or later-stage access, so it is a strong candidate for watch-listing rather than immediate deep research. Overall, the company looks like a high-upside, AI-native hardware play where fundamentals and AI score are strong, but return modeling and terms remain preliminary from public data alone.

*Confidence: medium*
