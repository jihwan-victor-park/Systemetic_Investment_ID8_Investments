**Rationale**

The deal passes the stage and geography gate: it is a Series B and the company is headquartered in Austin, Texas. It partially aligns with ID8’s thesis because the round includes a genuine new-money Tier 1 lead in TCGX, but it also includes ARCH as a founding/inside investor, so this is not a pure first-time new-money Tier 1-led round. Overall this looks like a strong but not perfect fit: compelling lead quality and late-stage biotech upside, but no AI exposure and no disclosed operating metrics or terms.

*Confidence: high*
