**Rationale**

Ollin Biosciences is a U.S.-based clinical-stage ophthalmology biotech headquartered in Austin, TX, raising a large, oversubscribed **Series B** round, so it passes both the stage and geography gates.[[4]](https://ollin.bio/press-releases/ollin-biosciences-announces-oversubscribed-330-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-global-phase-3-development-of-oln324-in-dme-and-wet-amd-studies-commencing-in-second-half-of-2026/)[[9]](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ollin-raises-330-million-fund-late-stage-eye-disease-drug-trials-2026-06-24/) The deal strongly aligns with ID8’s core thesis: the round is co-led by **new-money Tier 1 crossover investor TCGX** alongside inside/founding investor ARCH, in a clean Series B primary financing, and backed by a top-tier syndicate including T. Rowe Price, Blackstone, RA Capital, and Monograph.[[3]](https://trial.medpath.com/news/ollin-biosciences-secures-330m-series-b-to-advance-vabysmo-challenger-into-phase-3-trials)[[4]](https://ollin.bio/press-releases/ollin-biosciences-announces-oversubscribed-330-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-global-phase-3-development-of-oln324-in-dme-and-wet-amd-studies-commencing-in-second-half-of-2026/)[[6]](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/24/ophthalmology-biotech-ollin-biosciences/) While there is no AI element and commercial fundamentals are not yet visible, the scale of capital, Phase 3-ready lead asset in major ophthalmic indications, and high-quality investor roster support meaningful return potential with typical biotech clinical risk.

*Confidence: high*
