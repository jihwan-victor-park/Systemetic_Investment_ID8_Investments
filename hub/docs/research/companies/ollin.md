**Rationale**

Ollin Biosciences is an Austin-based, clinical-stage ophthalmology biotech raising a very large, oversubscribed **Series B** led by insider ARCH Venture Partners and new-money tier-1 crossover TCGX, with a strong syndicate of a16z Bio+Health, RA Capital, Blackstone, CPP Investments, and T. Rowe Price accounts, which aligns well with ID8’s emphasis on new-money tier-1 leads but only partially, since one co-lead is an existing investor rather than purely new-money.[[1]](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/ollin-biosciences-financing-china-innovent-vabysmo-amd-dme/823564/)[[2]](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/24/ophthalmology-biotech-ollin-biosciences/)[[4]](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/ollin-biosciences-raises-330m-series-b-for-eye-disease-drug/)[[9]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/innovents-partner-ollin-biosciences-announces-oversubscribed-330-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-global-phase-3-development-of-ibi324-oln324-in-dme-and-wet-amd-studies-commencing-in-second-half-of-2026-302809651.html) The company is pre-commercial and AI-light, but targets large, validated retinal markets with a Phase 3-ready asset, giving solid upside with material clinical risk; overall it fits ID8’s **stage and geography gates** and broadly matches the thesis on round dynamics, while fundamentals and AI exposure are weak under this rubric.

*Confidence: medium*
