**Rationale**

Ollin Biosciences is an Austin-based, clinical-stage ophthalmology biotech raising a large, oversubscribed $330M Series B to fund global Phase 3 trials of OLN324 in DME and wet AMD, with a strong crossover-heavy syndicate co-led by new investor TCGX and existing Tier 1 life-sciences investor ARCH.[[1]](https://ollin.bio/press-releases/ollin-biosciences-announces-oversubscribed-330-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-global-phase-3-development-of-oln324-in-dme-and-wet-amd-studies-commencing-in-second-half-of-2026/)[[3]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/innovents-partner-ollin-biosciences-announces-oversubscribed-330-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-global-phase-3-development-of-ibi324-oln324-in-dme-and-wet-amd-studies-commencing-in-second-half-of-2026-302809651.html)[[4]](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/ollin-biosciences-financing-china-innovent-vabysmo-amd-dme/823564/) The deal partially aligns with ID8’s thesis in that it is a sizable Series B with Tier 1 participation and a new-money top-tier(ish) co-lead, but it is not a pure new-money Tier 1-led round since ARCH is a founding/inside investor and co-lead, and the business is pre-revenue with high binary risk typical of late-stage biotech.[[1]](https://ollin.bio/press-releases/ollin-biosciences-announces-oversubscribed-330-million-series-b-financing-to-advance-global-phase-3-development-of-oln324-in-dme-and-wet-amd-studies-commencing-in-second-half-of-2026/)[[4]](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/ollin-biosciences-financing-china-innovent-vabysmo-amd-dme/823564/)[[6]](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ollin-raises-330-million-fund-late-stage-eye-disease-drug-trials-2026-06-24/) Overall fit is driven by attractive upside in large retinal markets and strong investors but constrained by lack of AI, limited financial fundamentals data, and the inside/outsider co-lead structure versus ID8’s ideal of a clean new-money Tier 1 lead.
