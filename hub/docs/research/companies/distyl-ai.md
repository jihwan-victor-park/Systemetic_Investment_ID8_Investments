**Rationale**

Distyl AI is a San Francisco–headquartered enterprise GenAI company squarely within ID8’s North America geography mandate and appears to be raising/has recently raised a large Series B+/growth round led by Tier 1+ Lightspeed with Sequoia participating, matching the thesis of new-money Tier 1-led B/C AI deals.[[1]](https://exa.ai/websets/directory/distyl-ai-offices)[[3]](https://lsvp.com/stories/investing-in-distyl-transforming-enterprises-with-genai/)[[5]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/distyl-ai-raises-175-million-at-1-8-billion-valuation-to-help-global-enterprises-become-ai-native-302564270.html) The team has strong AI and enterprise credentials, the product is a pure-play AI platform deeply integrated into customer workflows, and traction/valuation suggest substantial scale even though detailed financials are undisclosed; this supports solid Fundamentals and Return Potential but not yet at the "6x as base case" bar.
