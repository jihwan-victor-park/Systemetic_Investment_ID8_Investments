**Rationale**

Assort Health is a San Francisco–based, Series C-stage, pure-play healthcare AI agents platform, so it passes ID8’s stage and developed-market geography gates and sits squarely in the target Series C universe.[[3]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/assort-health-raises-120-million-series-c-to-scale-largest-deployment-of-ai-agents-for-the-patient-journey-302808634.html)[[8]](https://forgeglobal.com/assort-health_stock/) The deal broadly aligns with ID8’s thesis in that Menlo Ventures is leading a new-money Series C with a strong syndicate, but Menlo is more ‘near-top-tier’ than clearly Tier 1, so fit to the strict ‘new-money Tier 1 lead’ requirement is partial rather than perfect.[[3]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/assort-health-raises-120-million-series-c-to-scale-largest-deployment-of-ai-agents-for-the-patient-journey-302808634.html)[[9]](https://menlovc.com/perspective/keeping-the-front-door-of-healthcare-open-our-investment-in-assort-health/) Fundamentals and terms are not well disclosed publicly, so while the AI and market positioning look attractive and suggest solid return potential, the lack of hard operating metrics and deal-structure detail should keep this in a moderate- to high-quality band pending deeper diligence.
